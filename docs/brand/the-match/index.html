JAWNIX

Ping. Bid. Post.

We buy attention, turn a form or a call into a lead, and sell that lead to one buyer or a few — in real time. Not a hotel. Not a soap film. A match.

JAWNIX is always all caps. Exclusive, shared, or return. Never “hot.” Returns are credits, not “didn’t close.”

01

What this book is

The identity of the desk. The product that ships this language is a batch allocation desk — Batch Request, Lead Report, Disposition, Allocation. Do not relabel those screens Ping / Bid / Post.

The pipe in this book is real: a ping (ZIP + vertical, no name) fans out, buyers bid in 1800ms, the winner gets a post into their CRM. Exclusive is one buyer. Shared is two to five. That is how the trade talks. The mark is that pipe as a plate. The website uses the plate as color and chrome. It keeps the product nouns on screen.

Regenerated 17 Aug 2026 from the accepted law. The cloud originals lived at /root/jawnix/brand-books/01-the-match/. This folder is the copy in git. Do not invent a third mark.

04

Mark

One silhouette. Not three nodes with hairline joints.

Module M=8. Plate 14M × 6M. Left chamber: ping window 4M × 5M, Ping. Middle: bid hole, radius 1M, Bid. Right: post mass 6M, Ink. Walls 0.5M. ViewBox padded 2.4 so strokes are not clipped. Below 48px use mark-sm.svg (same plate, 0.75M walls). Clear space = one ping chamber.
