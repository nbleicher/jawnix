02

What we actually sell

product

A consumer hits submit on a page we paid for or own. We sell that inquiry to an operator who can take the job. The operator pays per lead, sometimes per call. That is the whole company.

How the lead is born

Paid search, SEO, display, partners. The consumer is in-market: a roof, an auto policy, a loan, a case. They fill a form or they call. We do not sell a “journey.” We sell the record that form created, or the live transfer of that call.

Who buys

Insurance agents and carriers. Home-service contractors. Legal intake. Education. Lenders. Call centers. They set ZIP, hours, vertical, exclusive vs shared, and a bid cap. They take delivery in a CRM, not on a letterhead.

Formats

  • Form fill — name, phone, email, ZIP, vertical, timestamp, consent
  • Inbound call / live transfer — same data, plus talk time
  • Click — used rarely; we are a lead desk first

Exclusive, shared, remnant

Exclusive: one buyer. Higher CPL. Hold 18 hours from post. Shared: two to five buyers get the same record. No hold. Speed-to-lead 60 seconds or the unit is waste. Remnant (R): leftover after a failed exclusive — 2 hours, then scrap. Ask which path you bought. Get it in writing.

What we do not sell

Aged lists we pretend are fresh. “Hot.” A handshake photo. A second copy of an exclusive. A name after TTL with the clock reset. If the number is disconnected, out of area, or missing consent, it is a return — not a story.

Category peers run the same machine under friendlier nouns. We use the nouns the buyer already uses on a ping tree.

03

The pipe

ping-post

Most internet leads are sold in two HTTP calls. Ping: enough to bid, not enough to call. Post: the full record, only to who paid.

Ping

// no name, no phone, no email
{
  "id": "nx-4418",
  "zip": "60614",
  "vertical": "home.roof",
  "source": "form"
}

Fan-out to every buyer whose filter matches. Window is 1800ms. A timeout is a no-bid. Losers never see PII.

Post

{
  "id": "nx-4418",
  "name": "R. Alvarez",
  "phone": "+1-312-555-0140",
  "email": "r.a@example.com",
  "consent_ts": "2026-08-17T00:14:01Z",
  "buyer": "northline",
  "exclusive": true,
  "price_usd": 240,
  "ttl_start": "2026-08-17T00:14:02Z",
  "ttl_hours": 18
}

Webhook or REST into the buyer CRM. Shared posts the same body to N winners at N prices.

Bid response · 1800ms

{
  "id": "nx-4418",
  "buyer": "northline",
  "price_usd": 240,
  "exclusive": true,
  "reject": null
}
// reject: "cap" | "hours" | "filter" · timeout = no body

No body in 1800ms is a timeout = no-bid. Tree ranks exclusive bids against the sum of shared bids, then posts to the winner. If that post does not ACK 2xx, try the next bid in rank. Losers never see PII.

Call / transfer post

{
  "id": "nx-4420",
  "source": "transfer",
  "talk_s": 94,
  "duration_s": 112,
  "disposition": "connected",
  "consent_ts": "2026-08-17T00:22:14Z",
  "exclusive": true,
  "ttl_start": "2026-08-17T00:22:18Z",
  "ttl_hours": 18
}

Same ping (no PII). Same bid window. Post adds talk time, duration, disposition. A transfer without talk_s does not post.

Platforms the category already runs: ping trees, boberdoo-class routers, Phonexa-class call. We do not need their logos. We need their facts on our screens.

04

Voice

desk

Write like the log. Named IDs. Dollars. Filters. No “empower.” No “journey.”

Use

  • Lead, inquiry, form fill, live transfer
  • Ping, bid, post, ACK, tree, filter
  • Exclusive, shared, remnant, return
  • CPL, bid cap, ZIP, vertical, TTL, speed-to-lead
  • Consent timestamp, TCPA, DNC

Refuse

  • Hot, crush, nurture, delight
  • As found, courtyard, capsule-as-poetry
  • Trusted partner, your journey starts here
  • Any line that would run on a homeowner landing page

To a buyer

Twelve exclusive home/roof leads, inbound form, ZIP 606xx. $240 CPL. 18-hour exclusive from post. Posted to your webhook. Shared from the same tree are a separate line at $42, speed-to-lead 60s. Remnant is a third line at $18, 2h then scrap. Returns: disconnected, wrong ZIP, no consent. We do not reset TTL.

Internal

nx-4418 won exclusive at 240. Cap blocked summit. Lakeview timed out. Media cost on the click path was 91. Keep the tree on exclusive until shared sum beats it.

Logo

mark + JAWNIX

The name is JAWNIX — one word, always all caps. The mark is a 14M × 6M plate: ping window, bid hole, post mass. Buyer-facing surfaces use lockup A. D (wordmark only) is allowed only when the mark is already in the chrome.

logo.svg is the light master. PNG masters are knockout (alpha in the window and hole) — do not bake #F4F6F7 into the punches. Files are outlined DM Sans 600, opsz 40, tracking −0.015em. Pair kerns: JA −0.02, AW −0.055, WN −0.02, NI −0.055, IX −0.03. Hostnames stay lowercase: jawnix.com.

Construction

The mark is a 14M × 6M plate (5M ping window, 3M bid hole, 6M post mass). M = 8. Wall 0.5M. Stroke 2.4. Ticks at 5M and 8M. The word is JAWNIX, cap 56, tracking −0.015em plus the pair table. Gap from plate to word is 28. Below 48 px the plate is the whole mark — do not set live type. Use mark-sm.svg under 64 px.

Lockups A–D

Dashed yard = clear space of one mark height. Primary lockup is A (plate + JAWNIX). C is the plate. D is the one-word mark.

08

The lead

record

A lead is a row with a clock. If a field is empty, it does not post.

Required to post

  • Name, phone or email, ZIP
  • Vertical + source (form / call / transfer)
  • Consent timestamp
  • Buyer, price, exclusive|shared
  • TTL start

Grades we actually use

A exclusive inbound form, filters clean. B exclusive, partner or call. C shared. R remnant after a failed exclusive — 2h then scrap. Returns are not a grade. They are a credit.

TTL and speed

Exclusive hold is 18 hours from post. Shared has no hold — speed-to-lead 60 seconds or the unit is waste. Remnant (R) lives 2 hours, then scrap. After-hours hold (filter hold=true) is a queue, not a ping: the form sits until the desk opens, then the tree pings, then TTL starts. A hold is not a second exclusive clock.

Returns

Disconnected, wrong geography, duplicate inside 18h exclusive / 24h shared, missing consent. Not “didn’t close.” Closing is their job. Data is ours.

Buyer filters

rules

The tree only pings buyers who can take the job. A filter miss is silence, not a discount.

FilterWhat it doesIf it fails
ZIP / radiusService areaNo ping
VerticalRoof ≠ HVACNo ping
HoursDesk openDefault: no ping. hold=true queues until open; TTL starts at post, not at form. See §08.
CapDaily $ or unit capBid rejected
Exclusive flagOne buyer vs NDifferent price path
Min bidFloor on the treeNo post

Shared vs exclusive is compared per lead: one exclusive bid against the sum of shared bids. The tree picks revenue, then posts. The buyer sees only their path.
